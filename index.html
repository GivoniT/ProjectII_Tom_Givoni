What to notice

  • Government sectors enrol the majority of students across every state.

Student to Teacher Ratios

Compare staffing ratios across sectors for primary and secondary schools. Use the dropdown to switch levels.

What to notice

  • Independent schools tend to run with lower student–teacher ratios than government schools.
  • Primary ratios are higher than secondary in most states, reflecting class size differences.
  • Switch between levels to spot states where staffing loads have shifted.
