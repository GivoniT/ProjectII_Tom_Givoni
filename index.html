Schools provide kids with a place to grow, connect, learn about the world around them and have fun. Facilitating this crucial step in human development are the many teachers (and staff) who roam the school. Student to teacher ratios are known as a good indicator of how much responsibility a teacher has, and are inversely proportional to how much attention they can give each kid. NAPLAN is a measure which is used to evaluate a student's performance. NAPLAN testing occurs annually, across Australia, for kids in years 3, 5, 7 and 9. They take tests in Spelling, Reading, Writing, Numeracy and Grammar. Let's see how males and females stack up across different states.

2025 NAPLAN Domain Scores by Gender

Toggle between national and state results to compare male and female average scores across NAPLAN domains for Years 3, 5, 7, and 9.

What to notice

  • Females lead Reading and Grammar scores in every state, while males typically edge ahead in Numeracy.
  • State gaps between genders widen in Writing, especially for the senior year levels.
  • Switch states to see how the overall score profile shifts across domains.

VCE

The Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE) is awarded to approximately 70% of students, and it is for most the natural path of growing up in Australia. Each student completes 5 to 6 subjects and receives a score out of 50 for each. These scores are added up and students are then ranked by percentile, which is referred to as their ATAR score. Let's view the distribution for the median study score for different schools, across Victoria.
